🩺 genlayer-doctor

Pre-deploy checks and “did my transaction really succeed?” diagnostics for GenLayer Intelligent Contracts. Runs entirely in your browser against the network's JSON-RPC. No wallet, no gas. CLI & source

Header lint (GLD001–008) and a gasless dry run: the node loads your contract with gen_getContractSchemaForCode and either returns its ABI or the GenVM error, which is decoded into a readable cause.

Examples:

A transaction can be FINALIZED / MAJORITY_AGREE and still have done nothing: consensus means the validators agreed on the outcome, even when the outcome was an error. Paste a hash to see the execution result, decoded return/revert payload, votes and the deployed header.
